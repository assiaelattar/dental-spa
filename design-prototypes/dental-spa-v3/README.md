# Dental Spa — Website V3 concept

**Creative direction:** Clinical Precision × Spa-Level Comfort. Refined Concept B / Sculpted Enamel branding.

## How to open

Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge or Safari. Routes use hash links and work without a web server:
- `#accueil` — complete homepage
- `#expertises` — areas of practice
- `#technologies` — digital dentistry
- `#docteur` — Dr Amina profile
- `#reservation` — three-step **demo booking UX**

## What is interactive

- Responsive mobile menu and SPA navigation
- Technology diagnostic-phase tabs with animated illustrative 3D model
- Staggered/reveal animations, scanning line, button and card micro-interactions
- Patient pathway selector
- Step-by-step mobile-first appointment demonstration with dynamic illustrative dates, input validation and accessible state changes
- Reduced-motion preference support

## Important integrity rules

- Doctor pictures are based on user-provided, original Dr Amina photographs (the editorial photograph is lightly brightness/contrast adjusted, not an AI composite).
- `dental_arch.svg` is an artistic/scientific illustration of a dental arch, not a patient scan or actual diagnostic view.
- The calendar shows **illustrative time slots only**. The demonstration does not transmit data or create a booking.
- The concept does not invent patient reviews, medical success rates or awards.
- Website statements regarding the practitioner and services draw on existing dentalspa.ma pages; verify wording with Dr Amina prior to publishing.
- The brand symbol has been recreated as a working SVG approximation of **Concept B** and requires final vector sign-off.
